# ObaaSima

*Obaa sima* — in Asante Twi, a mature, graceful woman.

A private period and pregnancy companion. Know your body, embrace your journey.

- Cycle tracking with predictions that learn from your own history
- Fertile window and ovulation estimates
- Daily health log: feelings, flow, symptoms, temperature, notes
- Pregnancy journey with week-by-week baby size
- Wellness tips for each phase of your cycle
- Works offline and installs to your home screen

**Privacy:** everything is stored only on your device. No accounts, ads, analytics or servers.

Predictions are estimates. ObaaSima is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception.
